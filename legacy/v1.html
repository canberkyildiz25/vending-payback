How the numbers are worked out

  • Monthly sales today = machines × sales per machine per day × selling days.
  • Extra gross profit = monthly sales today × sales increase × gross margin. Only the margin counts as gain, not the full extra revenue.
  • Card processing fees apply to the card and phone share of the new, higher sales.
  • Net gain per month = extra gross profit + cash handling saved − processing fees − reader service fees.
  • Payback period = reader price for the whole fleet ÷ net gain per month.

The sales increase and the card share differ by location and audience. Use figures from your own pilot machines where you have them.
